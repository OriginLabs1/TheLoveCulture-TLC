TLC 0.0.3 keeps the record.

The preview is over: recognition you write down now survives closing the app, rebooting the phone
and installing an update. The record is a readable text file in the app's private folder, and every
launch re-checks it before the app will add another line.

### What changed

- **The record is durable.** Entries are written with `fsync` before the app says they were kept. A
  crash mid-write is repaired on the next launch by dropping only the incomplete line. Appends are
  refused if the record does not verify, so damage cannot be buried under new history.
- **The journal is real.** A calm, readable list of who showed up and what they did, newest first,
  with search, a per-entry proof sheet, and a seal you can copy or share.
- **Verification is visible.** The Verify screen recomputes the chain, shows the head hash and Merkle
  root, distinguishes a damaged record from a truncated one, and exports the exact text the
  `ledger-verify` CLI reads.
- **A record you can leave with.** Export writes the whole record through the system file picker.
  Share sends a seal — entry count, head, root and time — so a later copy can be compared against it.
- **Still no network.** The APK requests no `INTERNET` permission, contains no Play Services, no
  Firebase and no analytics. Nothing leaves the device unless you export or share it.

### Two kinds of damage, named honestly

A tail lost on this device is detected by a local seal (`length:head:root`). A line that was edited
is detected by recomputing every hash. A record that fails either check opens read-only with a plain
explanation, rather than pretending everything is fine.

### What this build does not do yet

There is no account, no sync and no multi-device merge. Steward approvals, dyads and growth grants
are the next milestone. Tokens remain recognition signals — not currency, not transferable, not
redeemable, not crypto — and TLC still never holds, moves or escrows money.

### Proven by the build, not by the README

- 89 invariant tests across the domain state machine, the ledger, the file format, crash recovery
  and the command-line verifier.
- Simulated disk failures, torn writes, deleted tails, edited amounts, rewritten hashes and
  duplicated idempotency keys are all covered by tests.
- The `foss` channel is checked for Play Services, Firebase and tracking dependencies.
- The release APK is checked for the absence of the `INTERNET` permission and of Play Services
  classes in its dex, and its signing certificate is pinned to the project's key.

GPL-3.0-or-later. No analytics, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking.

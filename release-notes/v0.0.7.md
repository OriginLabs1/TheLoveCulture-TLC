TLC 0.0.7 — The door, the face and the route.

v0.0.6 made the relationship visible. v0.0.7 makes it yours to enter. A distinct username and a
word/phrase passphrase now open the circle; a real avatar studio frames your face before it is
saved; presence is four separate choices that start at Nobody; and a token transfer asks which
real route the value will take. There is no fingerprint, no face unlock, no passkey and no device
shortcut anywhere in this build — the passphrase is the only door.

Still local-only. Still no account on a server, no sync, no multi-device merge, no `INTERNET`
permission. The presence controls are stored on this device and are the instruction the shared
plane must obey when it arrives. The Transfer Method sheet describes real routes honestly; it does
not invent a provider quote this build cannot see.

### The door

- **A distinct username.** Choose a handle once, see it on your profile, and use it at the door.
  Reserved handles are refused.
- **A word/phrase passphrase.** Four ordinary words or one long line. The UI shows an honest shape,
  never a shaming score. A salt and a PBKDF2 verifier stay on the device; the passphrase itself is
  never stored, never logged and never sent.
- **No biometrics. Ever.** No fingerprint prompt, no face prompt, no passkey sheet, no
  device-credential fallback. The copy gate and an architecture gate fail the build if a biometric
  or passkey API appears in the Android source.
- **Lock, sign out, recover.** Lock now returns to the passphrase screen. Sign out returns to the
  door and leaves the record on the device. A forgotten passphrase can be reset only by starting a
  fresh circle on this device; export a bundle first if you want to keep the record.
- **Upgrade path.** A device with a v0.0.6 record but no passphrase is asked once to set the words,
  without discarding the record.

### The face

- **Avatar studio.** Pick a photo, then drag, pinch or use the slider to frame it inside the circle
  you will actually see. The preview and the saved file share one geometry, so heads are no longer
  cropped off by a silent centre crop.
- **A monogram instead of an empty box.** With no photo, your initials sit on a quiet blue-to-purple
  gradient. No bundled placeholder, no broken-image square.
- **A profile that reads like a person.** A gradient hero, an overlapping ringed avatar, an edit
  badge, three honest counters, your details, your photo, your presence summary and the security
  of the door — in one calm column.
- **Metadata-minimised.** The framed image is downscaled, re-encoded without EXIF and kept in the
  app's private folder as a file name and a SHA-256 fingerprint. The bytes never enter the state
  file or the record.

### Who can see you

- **Four signals, four independent choices.** Online now, last seen, typing and read receipts each
  choose their own audience: Nobody, Selected people, My circle or Everyone.
- **Private by default.** Every signal starts at Nobody. Invisible overrides everything.
- **Selected and hidden people.** Add a username to either list. Hidden wins even when another
  setting would share.
- **Exact last seen is a separate choice.** When off, the other side sees a coarse window, never a
  timestamp.
- **A live preview.** Pick a person and see exactly what they would see, signal by signal. Silence
  is never rendered as "offline".
- **Honest about the boundary.** The choices are stored on this device now; the shared plane applies
  exactly these choices when it is connected.

### The route

- **A Transfer Method sheet inside the transfer.** Not a setting, not a buried preference: choose
  the route while you are sending.
- **Real descriptors.** Provider, fee, who pays, arrival, limit and availability, in plain words.
- **Two routes are usable today.** A direct bank transfer and an instant bank rail. Card-funded,
  cash-agent and international routes are shown disabled with the reason, rather than invented.
- **A held route window.** The chosen method stays visible with a two-minute window and a one-tap
  way to switch. The provider confirms the exact fee and timing when you leave TLC.
- **Still not a bank.** TLC never holds or moves the money, shows no balance and never calls a
  claim a settlement. You complete the route in your own bank or licensed provider and bring back
  the reference.

### The surface

- New typography scale, calmer spacing and a consistent shape language across the new screens.
- A single passphrase notice, a single no-biometrics notice, and a single instrument notice — worded
  the same wherever they appear.
- Every new screen is reachable by screen reader, with content descriptions on the controls that
  carry meaning and colour never the only signal.
- Motion still respects the system setting and the in-app preference.

### What has not changed

- **The record is still the record.** Append-only, hash-chained, flushed before the app says it was
  kept, re-verified on every launch, read-only when it does not check out.
- **Still no network.** No `INTERNET` permission, no Play Services, no Firebase, no analytics, no
  proprietary SDK.
- **Still no custody.** No wallet, no balance, no escrow, no payment rail, no card data. No
  recurring charge is shown to anyone, anywhere.
- **Still no shared plane.** Accounts on a server, encrypted messaging, cross-device presence and
  Pear replication are the next milestone, not this one.

### Proven by the build, not by the README

- `make verify` runs the domain, experience and ledger suites; the state round-trip and migrations
  still pass.
- `make copy-lint` scans every user-facing surface and every Android source file. It fails on
  banned copy and on any biometric or passkey API.
- The `foss` channel is checked for Play Services, Firebase and tracking dependencies.
- The release APK is checked for the absence of the `INTERNET` permission and Play Services classes
  in its dex, and its signing certificate is pinned to the project's key.

### Where it ships

The same signed `tlc-0.0.7-foss.apk` is the artifact for GitHub Releases, F-Droid, Orion Store and
Uptodown. GitHub Releases is the source of truth. No channel re-signs it: one package id, one
signature, one update path.

GPL-3.0-or-later. No analytics, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking.

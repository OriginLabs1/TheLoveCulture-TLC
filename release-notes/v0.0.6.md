TLC 0.0.6 — Tokens, receipts & faces.

v0.0.5 proved the relationship. v0.0.6 makes it visible. One Tokens surface shows every Support,
Pioneer's Token and Growth Grant you offered or received, with its real state, its timeline and its
receipt. Your profile is now yours to shape, and a receipt is an image you own rather than a claim
the app makes for you.

Still local-only. Still no account, no sync, no multi-device merge. Still no `INTERNET` permission.
This release is honest about the boundary: the Received view shows what is actually on this device
and never invents a number.

### Tokens, in one place

- **One surface for every movement.** Support, Pioneer's Token and Growth Grant share a single
  list with an **All / Offered / Received** segment, so the thing you are looking for is never in a
  different screen from the last time you saw it.
- **Counters, not a balance.** Three counters — circulation, transferred, received — animate
  when they change and describe movement only. There is no balance, no ranking and no pooled total.
- **Every token keeps its own story.** Tap a token to see its kind, its person, its amount if one
  was stated, its bank reference, and the honest timeline from offer to acknowledgement. The
  screen always names the next honest action instead of implying that money moved.
- **A circulation strip on Today.** The same three counters appear on the home screen, so circulation is
  visible without opening a second screen.
- **Received tells the truth.** On this device you see what you offered and what you recorded.
  Tokens sent from another device arrive when the shared plane ships; until then the Received view
  is empty by design, with a note that says why.

### Receipts you own

- **Attach, replace, remove.** Add a photo or screenshot to any Support, Pioneer's Token or Growth
  Grant. One current image per token: replacing it replaces it, removing it removes it, and the
  record keeps the fact.
- **Metadata-minimised by default.** The image is downscaled, re-encoded as JPEG, stripped of EXIF,
  centre-cropped where appropriate, and stored in the app's private folder as a file name plus a
  SHA-256 fingerprint. Image bytes never enter the state file or the record.
- **Visibility is explicit and revocable.** Choose **Only me**, **This transfer**, **My circle**,
  **Anyone with the link** or **Public in Record**. The default for a token is This transfer, and
  changing it is a recorded event.
- **Sharing is a user action.** Sharing a receipt opens the system chooser through a narrow
  `FileProvider` grant; nothing leaves the device until you choose to send it.
- **Open it full size.** Tap the receipt to view the image, with its fingerprint underneath.

### Your face, your name

- **Profile photos.** Pick a photo with the Android system picker, see it previewed, replace it or
  remove it at any time. It is re-encoded without metadata and kept in private storage.
- **A name you can change.** Update your display name and circle name without losing your handle,
  your record or your tokens. Saving is a separate act, so an accidental edit cannot change the
  circle.
- **Your avatar on Today.** Tap your own face to open your profile.

### A more restrained colour language

- **Forest green** means received, verified and growth.
- **Royal purple** means Pioneer, stewardship and recognition.
- Both are accents only: never a surface wash, never the primary action, and never the only signal.
  Every state is also spelled out in words and reachable without colour.

### Motion, selectively

- Counters animate to their new value, cards reveal on arrival, and taps give a short, calm
  response. Motion respects the system setting and the in-app preference; turning it off removes
  the animation without removing information.

### What has not changed

- **The record is still the record.** Append-only, hash-chained, flushed before the app says it was
  kept, re-verified on every launch, and read-only when it does not check out. v0.0.6 adds
  `PROFILE_UPDATED`, `RECEIPT_ADDED`, `RECEIPT_REMOVED` and `RECEIPT_AUDIENCE_CHANGED` as facts.
- **Still no network.** No `INTERNET` permission, no Play Services, no Firebase, no analytics, no
  proprietary SDK.
- **Still no custody.** No wallet, no balance, no escrow, no payment rail, no card data. The app
  records the intent, the instruction and the evidence; the transfer happens bank to bank.
- **Still no account.** A Co-Steward is a local persona. Accounts, sync, encrypted messaging and
  the shared plane are the next milestone, not this one.

### Proven by the build, not by the README

- The domain, experience and ledger suites cover profile updates, receipt attach/replace/remove,
  audience changes, orphan receipts, state round-trip and the v1 → v2 → v3 migration. The state
  file carries file names and fingerprints, never image bytes.
- The `foss` channel is checked for Play Services, Firebase and tracking dependencies.
- The release APK is checked for the absence of the `INTERNET` permission and of Play Services
  classes in its dex, and its signing certificate is pinned to the project's key.

### Where it ships

The same signed `tlc-0.0.6-foss.apk` is the artifact for GitHub Releases, F-Droid, Orion Store and
Uptodown. GitHub Releases is the source of truth. The F-Droid metadata is pinned to this commit and
this APK; Orion Store reads the same GitHub release; Uptodown takes the same file through its
developer console. No channel re-signs it: one package id, one signature, one update path.

GPL-3.0-or-later. No analytics, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking.

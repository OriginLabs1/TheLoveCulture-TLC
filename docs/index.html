What this build does

  • Onboarding, a local profile and a @handle, then Today, Record, People, Tokens, Settings and a Create sheet.
  • An append-only, hash-chained record that is re-verified on every launch and opens read-only if it does not check out.
  • One Tokens surface for every Support, Pioneer's Token and Growth Grant, with live circulation counters and an honest timeline.
  • Receipt images you own: downscaled, metadata-stripped, stored privately, and shared only with an audience you choose.
  • Your own face and name: pick or replace a profile photo, edit your display name, and keep your handle and record.
  • Names for people and roles: @pioneer is the official role and @sircollins is the person behind it.
  • An encrypted bank vault: save a destination once, see only the last four digits, and reveal the full number only on purpose.
  • Send Support, Pioneer's Token, a purpose-first Pioneer Desk, Growth Grants with evidence and witness stages.
  • Verified backup and restore, Merkle proofs per entry, and an export the ledger-verify tool reads.

What it will never do

TLC never holds, pools, escrows or moves money. There is no wallet, no balance, no payment rail and no card data. A transfer happens bank to bank between two private accounts; TLC records the intent, the instruction and the evidence.

A full bank account number is never compiled in and never enters the record. It lives only in the payer's encrypted vault, protected by the Android Keystore; the app shows the bank name, the last four digits and a fingerprint, and reveals the rest only on an explicit action.

Direction for v0.0.7: accounts, sync and the social layer

The unit of TLC's Standard Economic Framework is the Token: a record of a meaningful resource, item, gift or monetary value. The non-custody line does not change — a Token is a record of value, not a balance, and settlement stays bank to bank. This is recorded in docs/SEF.md. Accounts, cross-device sync, the remote attestation desk and the social layer are the next milestone, not a promise about this build.

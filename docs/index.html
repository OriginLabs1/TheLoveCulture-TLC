What this build does

  • Onboarding and a local profile, then Today, Journal, Grants, Dues, Circle, Settings and About.
  • An append-only, hash-chained record that is re-verified on every launch and opens read-only if it does not check out.
  • Growth Grants with evidence and witness stages; Pioneer dues that stop at proof submission.
  • Verified backup and restore, Merkle proofs per entry, and an export the ledger-verify tool reads.

What it will never do

TLC never holds, pools, escrows or moves money. There is no wallet, no balance, no payment rail and no card data. A transfer happens bank to bank between two private accounts; TLC records the intent, the instruction and the evidence.

Pioneer bank details are not in this build and are never compiled in. When the fee flow ships, they are released only to the payer, only after the due exists, for the reason the due describes, and they expire.

Direction for v0.0.5: the SEF Token

The unit of TLC's Standard Economic Framework is the Token: a record of a meaningful resource, item, gift or monetary value. Tokens may be curated, transferred or distributed in monetary form for convenience. The non-custody line does not change — a Token is a record of value, not a balance, and settlement stays bank to bank. This is recorded in docs/SEF.md and is not in v0.0.4; this build still describes tokens as recognition signals because the valuation and transfer rules do not exist yet.

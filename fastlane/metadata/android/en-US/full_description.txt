TLC records recognition — who showed up for whom, and what they did — in a record that cannot be quietly rewritten, and that now survives closing the app.

It exists for one narrow purpose: to make care and contribution visible between people who trust each other, and to keep the memory of it beyond the reach of a silent edit.

WHAT THIS BUILD ACTUALLY DOES

• Keeps your record on the device. Entries are written to a readable text file in the app's private folder and flushed to disk before the app says they were kept.
• Re-checks the whole record on every launch. Each entry carries the SHA-256 hash of the one before it, so editing a past entry breaks every later hash.
• Names the damage. A tail lost on this device is reported as missing entries; an edited line is reported as a failed verification. A record that does not verify opens read-only instead of quietly accepting new history.
• Repairs an interrupted write by dropping only the incomplete line, then verifying what remains.
• Lets you search the journal, open any entry to see its Merkle proof against the current root, copy its hash, share a seal (length, head, root, time) or export the full record through the system file picker.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET

There is no account, no sync and no multi-device merge. Steward approvals, dyads and growth grants are the next milestone, not a promise about this build.

WHAT TLC WILL NEVER DO

• It never holds, moves, pools or escrows money. There is no wallet, no balance, no payment rail, no card data.
• The app shows where to send a payment and records the evidence you bring back. The money moves between two private bank accounts, bank to bank, and TLC is not a party to it.
• Recognition tokens are signals of appreciation: not currency, not transferable, not redeemable, not crypto.
• No analytics on amounts, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking.

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION

This build contains no networking code and its APK does not request the INTERNET permission. Nothing leaves the device unless you export or share it. There is no Google Play Services, no Firebase, no analytics and no proprietary SDK.

CHECK IT YOURSELF

Export the record, then run the ledger-verify command-line tool from the source repository. It recomputes every hash and reports the first break — the same check the app performs, outside the app.

WHO IT IS FOR

Families, small circles, community groups and cooperatives — anyone who wants a trustworthy record of who showed up for whom, without handing that record to a company.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE

GPL-3.0-or-later, no proprietary SDKs, no Google Play Services. The same signed build is published to GitHub Releases, F-Droid and Orion Store.

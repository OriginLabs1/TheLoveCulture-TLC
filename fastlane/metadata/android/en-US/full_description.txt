TLC is a preview of an app for recognising care and contribution between people who trust each other — and for keeping a record of it that cannot be quietly rewritten.

It exists for one narrow purpose: to make good work visible, and to make the record of it tamper-evident.

WHAT THIS PREVIEW BUILD ACTUALLY DOES

• Walks the activation flow: get details, bring back proof, receive an attestation, reach a receipt.
• Every step is decided by the same rules the tests cover — the screen has no second copy of them.
• Shows a hash-chained ledger: each entry carries the hash of the one before it, so editing history breaks verification.
• Works with the radio off. This build contains no networking code at all, and its APK does not request the INTERNET permission.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET

Be clear about this: version 0.0.2 is a preview. The flow uses demonstration data, the ledger lives in memory for the length of a session, and records are not yet kept between launches. There is no account, no sync and no sharing yet. On-device persistence and the two-steward flow are the next milestone, not a promise about this build.

WHAT TLC WILL NEVER DO

• It never holds, moves, pools or escrows money. There is no wallet, no balance, no payment rail, no card data.
• The app shows where to send a payment and records the evidence you bring back. The money moves between two private bank accounts, bank to bank, and TLC is not a party to it.
• Recognition tokens are signals of appreciation: not currency, not transferable, not redeemable, not crypto.
• No analytics on amounts, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking.

WHO IT IS FOR

Families, small circles, community groups and cooperatives — anyone who wants a trustworthy record of who showed up for whom, without handing that record to a company.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE

GPL-3.0-or-later, no proprietary SDKs, no Google Play Services. The same signed build is published to GitHub Releases, F-Droid and Orion Store.

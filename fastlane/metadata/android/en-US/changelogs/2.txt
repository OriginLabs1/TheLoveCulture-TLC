TLC 0.0.3 — the record is durable.

• Recognition now survives closing the app: entries are fsynced to a readable text file in the app's private folder.
• Every launch re-verifies the whole hash chain before adding anything; a damaged or truncated record opens read-only with a plain explanation.
• A crash mid-write is repaired by dropping only the incomplete line.
• New journal: search, per-entry Merkle proof, copy/share a seal, export the record.
• Verify screen shows the head hash, anchor root and exactly what failed.
• Still no INTERNET permission, no Play Services, no Firebase, no analytics. Tokens are recognition signals only.

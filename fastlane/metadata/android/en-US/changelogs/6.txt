TLC 0.0.6 — Tokens, receipts & faces.

• Tokens: one surface for every Support, Pioneer's Token and Growth Grant, with All / Sent / Received and a live timeline.
• Live counters for circulation, transferred and received. No balance, no ranking, no pooled total.
• Receipts: attach, replace, remove and open full size. Downscaled, metadata-stripped and stored in the app's private folder.
• Visibility: Only me, This transfer, My circle, Anyone with the link or Public in Record — explicit, revocable and recorded.
• Profile: pick, replace or remove a photo; edit your display name and circle without losing your handle or record.
• Forest green for received, verified and growth; royal purple for Pioneer, stewardship and recognition. Accents only.
• Selective motion: animated counters and reveals that respect the system and in-app settings.

Still local-only: no account, no sync, no multi-device merge. The Received view shows only what is on this device until the shared plane ships. Still no INTERNET permission, no Play Services, no Firebase and no analytics. No wallet, no balance, no escrow, no payment rail. Receipt and profile images never enter the state file or the record. The APK is signed with the project key and published unchanged to GitHub, F-Droid, Orion Store and Uptodown.
